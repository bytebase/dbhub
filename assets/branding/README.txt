Usage
Prefer SVG for websites and scalable artwork. Lettering is outlined; no fonts are required.
Scale proportionally. Preserve the logo's icon-to-wordmark size, spacing, and optical alignment.
The starfish is 136 units tall; lettering is 103 units tall; gap is 24 units.
The icon is optically raised 5 units relative to geometric center alignment.
Keep at least one quarter of the starfish height clear around the logo.
Use the complete logo files when displaying icon and wordmark together.
