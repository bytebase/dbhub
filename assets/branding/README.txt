DBHub — Aurora Bloom logo set

CONTENTS
- svg/: 9 scalable vector assets exported directly from Figma.
- png/512w/, png/1024w/, png/2048w/: the same 9 assets at each width.
- previews/: light/dark icon and full-logo presentation sheets.
- manifest.json: source node IDs, original dimensions, and export sizes.

CHOOSING AN ASSET
dbhub-icon-transparent: standalone Aurora Bloom; works on light and dark backgrounds.
dbhub-logo-for-light-background: transparent full logo with zinc DBHub lettering.
dbhub-logo-for-dark-background: transparent full logo with white DBHub lettering.
dbhub-wordmark-for-light-background: zinc custom DBHub lettering only.
dbhub-wordmark-for-dark-background: white custom DBHub lettering only.
Files named white-background or zinc-background include that background and padding.

FORMATS
SVG artwork is vector-only, including the custom lettering; no fonts are required.
PNG widths are 512, 1024, and 2048 pixels; heights preserve each asset's aspect ratio.
Transparent PNGs retain an alpha channel. Background variants are opaque.
PNGs were rendered from the native Figma SVG exports with librsvg.

APPROVED FULL-LOGO PROPORTIONS
Starfish visible height: 132 design units.
Wordmark height: 103 design units.
Icon/wordmark gap: 20 design units.
Visible centers are closely matched; both elements use vertical center alignment.
The horizontal transparent logo is approximately 546.589 × 132 design units.

AURORA BLOOM COLORS
Radial gradient: #B2FCCB (0%), #19DECD (25%), #2AA1FE (62%), #955BFE (100%).
Eyes and smile: #19253C.
Zinc lettering / dark background: #323236.
White lettering / light background: #FFFFFF.
